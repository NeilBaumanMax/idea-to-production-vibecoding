# Product Discovery Questionnaire

Do not ask every question mechanically. Select the smallest useful batch.

## A. Origin

- What experience caused this idea?
- What are users doing today instead?
- Why is the current solution unsatisfactory?
- Why is this worth building now?

## B. Primary User

- Who is the first real user?
- What skill level do they have?
- What environment or device do they use?
- Who is explicitly not the first-release target?

## C. Core Product Object

- What is the main object: project, skill, document, task, product, asset, workflow, profile, order, or something else?
- What supporting object must not become the main product?
- Can one core object contain children or bundles?
- Who owns, creates, edits, and publishes it?

## D. Main Journey

- What does the user see first?
- Why would they click?
- What information builds trust?
- What is the most important action?
- What proves value was delivered?

## E. First-Release Boundary

- What five capabilities are mandatory?
- What is explicitly excluded?
- Which future concepts only need reserved fields?
- What can remain manual?
- What must be automated?

## F. Content and Moderation

- Who can publish?
- Is submission open, reviewed, or administrator-only?
- How is attribution stored?
- What content can be downloaded or redistributed?
- What requires manual approval?

## G. Visual Direction

- Which three products inspire the experience?
- What should users feel?
- What should the product never look like?
- Is discovery driven by search, feeds, categories, or recommendations?
- What role do images, diagrams, video, and examples play?

## H. Technical Constraints

- Existing repository?
- Preferred CLI agent?
- Preferred stack?
- Package manager?
- Authentication requirement?
- File storage requirement?
- Search requirement?
- Deployment target?
- Expected first-release scale?

## I. Success

- What must be true for version one to be considered successful?
- Which three metrics matter?
- What user behavior proves the product is useful?
- What operational burden is acceptable?
